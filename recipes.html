🍋

Vegetable Poha

A light, tasty breakfast with vegetables.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup poha
  • Chopped onion and vegetables
  • Mustard seeds and curry leaves
  • Turmeric, salt and lemon

Instructions

Rinse the poha. Sauté mustard seeds, curry leaves, onion and vegetables. Add poha and seasoning. Mix gently and finish with lemon juice.

Healthy Chilla Recipes

✿
🥞
HIGH-PROTEIN

Besan Chilla

A savoury gram flour pancake with fresh vegetables.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup besan
  • Chopped onion and tomato
  • Fresh coriander
  • ½ teaspoon ajwain
  • Turmeric and salt
  • Water and a little oil

Instructions

Mix besan, spices, vegetables and enough water to make a smooth batter. Spread on a lightly greased pan. Cook both sides until golden and fully cooked.

🌿
PROTEIN-RICH

Moong Dal Chilla

A delicious savoury pancake made with moong dal.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup moong dal
  • Chopped onion
  • Fresh coriander
  • Ginger and green chilli (optional)
  • Salt to taste
  • Water and a little oil

Instructions

Soak the moong dal for a few hours, then drain and blend with a little water. Mix in the vegetables and seasoning. Spread the batter on a lightly greased pan. Cook both sides until golden and fully cooked.

🥗
FIBRE-RICH

Oats Chilla

A tasty oats pancake with crunchy vegetables.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup powdered oats
  • Chopped onion and capsicum
  • Fresh coriander
  • Salt and spices to taste
  • Water and a little oil

Instructions

Mix powdered oats, vegetables, spices and water to make a pourable batter. Spread it on a lightly greased pan. Cook both sides until golden and cooked through.

🧀
PANEER SPECIAL

Paneer Stuffed Chilla

A savoury chilla filled with spiced paneer.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup besan
  • ½ cup crumbled paneer
  • Chopped onion and coriander
  • Salt and spices to taste
  • Water and a little oil

Instructions

Prepare a smooth besan batter with water and seasoning. Separately mix paneer, onion and coriander. Cook the chilla on a lightly greased pan, add the paneer filling, fold and cook until done.

Moong Dal Khichdi

A comforting rice and lentil dish.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • ½ cup rice
  • ½ cup moong dal
  • Turmeric and salt
  • Water and optional vegetables

Instructions

Wash the rice and dal. Add them to a pressure cooker with water, turmeric, salt and vegetables. Cook until soft, following the cooker's instructions.
