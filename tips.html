THE WELLNESS JOURNAL

A Little Guide to Feeling Good

Explore simple tips for your everyday life.

🥗
HEALTHY EATING

Nourish Your Body

  • Try to include different fruits and vegetables in your meals.
  • Eat regular, balanced meals that you enjoy.
  • Keep easy, nourishing snacks available.
💧
HYDRATION

Stay Refreshed

  • Keep a water bottle nearby.
  • Drink water regularly throughout the day.
  • Have water when you feel thirsty.
🌙
BETTER SLEEP

Create a Calm Night

  • Try to follow a regular sleep schedule.
  • Make your sleeping space comfortable.
  • Enjoy a quiet activity before bedtime.
🧘🏻‍♀️
MENTAL WELLNESS

Give Your Mind a Break

  • Take short breaks when you feel overwhelmed.
  • Try slow, comfortable breathing.
  • Talk to someone you trust when you need support.
🧴
SKINCARE

Keep It Simple

  • Cleanse your face gently.
  • Use a moisturiser suitable for your skin.
  • Protect your skin with sunscreen during the day.
📚
STUDY & FOCUS

Make Study Time Easier

  • Break large tasks into smaller steps.
  • Keep your study space organised.
  • Take breaks and ask for help when needed.
🌿
ACTIVE LIVING

Move in Ways You Enjoy

  • Try a walk, gentle stretching or dancing.
  • Choose movement that feels comfortable.
  • Rest whenever your body needs it.
💗
SELF-KINDNESS

Be Gentle With Yourself

  • Celebrate the little things you accomplish.
  • Make time for activities you enjoy.
  • Remember that everyone has difficult days.
🌸

A Note to Remember

You are allowed to learn, grow, pause and begin again. Take life one day at a time.
