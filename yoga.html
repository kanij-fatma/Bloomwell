GENTLE PRACTICES

Explore Yoga Poses

1. Prayer Pose

Stand straight and join your palms in front of your chest.

2. Raised Arms Pose

Raise both arms gently above your head and stretch upwards.

3. Forward Bend

Bend forward gently and let your hands move towards your feet.

4. Cobra Pose

Lie on your stomach and gently lift your chest upwards.

5. Mountain Pose

Lift your hips gently and form an inverted V shape with your body.
